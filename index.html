Now it's time to begin a new chapter that uses all these skills and services but in a targeted objective of playing it forward, encouraging more women to lead in business, public policy, and politics. Please visit members from our team at the South Carolina Women's Leadership Network, or SC Women in Leadership (SC WIL). In this next chapter, we hope to move South Carolina forward – socially, economically, educationally, and environmentally – through information, inspiration, and involvement.

Thank you for your support and friendship.



SC Women in Leadership is a multi-partisan organization that promotes democracy by encouraging women to become informed about public issues and active in the public debate. WIL helps all those who express interest to find resources for information on those issues. WIL does not endorse particular views, legislation, or candidates for public office.
